A time series is a [Time window](/domain/concepts/time-window) that repeats on specific weekdays.

It is used in [time profiles](/domain/concepts/time-profile) to temporally restrict [Authorizations](/domain/concepts/authorizations).

### Example

Every Mo, Tue, Wed, Do, Fr from 08:00-17:00
