Specify access rights/privileges, respectively check them during operation to grant or reject access.

Authorization can be granted to an [Identification Medium](/domain/concepts/identification-medium) for 
* [Installation Points](/domain/concepts/installation-point)
* [Zones](/domain/concepts/zone)

and limited by [Time profiles](/domain/concepts/time-profile).

See also [Authorization@Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Authorization)
