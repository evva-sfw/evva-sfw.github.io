Is a form of digital credential within an access control system that can be issued to a [Person](/domain/concepts/person) and then used by the person for [Authentication](/domain/concepts/authentication) and [Authorization](/domain/concepts/authorization) 
at an [Access Component](/domain/concepts/access-component). 

A lost identification medium can be blocked using the [Blocklist](/domain/concepts/blocklist).

There exist two different types of identification media:
1. [RFID identification medium](/domain/concepts/rfid-identification-medium)
2. [Mobile identification medium](/domain/concepts/mobile-identification-medium)
