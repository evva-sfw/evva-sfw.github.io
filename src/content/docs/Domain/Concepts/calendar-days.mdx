A calendar day is a day on the standard Gregorian calendar that is in use worldwide for civil purposes.

It is used in [time series exceptions](/domain/concepts/time-series-exception) to provision special days with a different [Time Window](/domain/concepts/time-window)
than it would normally have on the given weekday of the [Time Series](/domain/concepts/time-series). The configuration is actually on the [Access component](/domain/concepts/access-component).


### Example

01.01.2026, 24.12.2026, 31.12.2026
