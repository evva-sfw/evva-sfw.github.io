Near field radio-frequency [identification media](/domain/concepts/identification-medium) that consist of a microchip an antenna and a substrate and come in different form factors like
* cards
* keyfobs
* bracelets 

For EVVA products these are currently only based on the [IEC 14443 Standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO/IEC_14443) and operate in 
HF at 13.56 MHz.
